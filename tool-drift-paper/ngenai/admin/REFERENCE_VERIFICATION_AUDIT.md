## Bottom line

- All **22** references rendered in `main.bbl` were independently located in an authoritative first-party record: the publisher's proceedings, ACL Anthology, PMLR, SpringerLink, arXiv, OpenReview, or the cited vendor's official documentation.
- **No hallucinated, nonexistent, or unverifiable reference was found.**
- **No materially wrong DOI, arXiv identifier, URL, author list, year, venue, or page range was found.**
- Three minor exact-metadata/style points are worth knowing but do not cast doubt on any source:
  1. `li2023apibank`: the rendered title is “API-bank”; the official title is “API-Bank” (capitalization only).
  2. `liu2024apigen`: the official NeurIPS display title uses the stylized spelling “Automated **PI**peline”; the bibliography normalizes this to “Automated Pipeline” (capitalization/styling only).
  3. `schick2023toolformer`: the paper PDF spells Roberto **Dessì** with an accent; the bibliography renders “Dessi” (minor author-name diacritic omission). The NeurIPS HTML itself also displays “Dessi,” so this is a publisher-record inconsistency rather than evidence of fabrication.
- The Notion and Stripe access dates are chronologically plausible: 2026-04-14 and 2026-07-29 both precede the audit date. Both official pages were reachable during this audit. A live page cannot, by itself, prove what its contents looked like on the historical access date.
- Citation-set consistency passed: the 22 unique keys cited in `main.tex` exactly match the 22 `\bibitem` keys rendered in `main.bbl`; there are no missing or extra rendered references.

## Method and status definitions

Each rendered entry was compared with a primary authoritative record. “Verified” means that existence, title, ordered authors, year, venue, pages (where applicable), and DOI/arXiv/URL agree, allowing ordinary bibliography-style capitalization and abbreviation. “Verified — minor” means the work and identifier are unquestionably real, with only the explicitly described non-substantive metadata difference. For OpenReview-hosted conference papers, an arXiv author record was also used when useful to corroborate the full ordered author list.
