pp. 19–23V - Vehicle Requirements

Defines requirements for an open-wheeled, open-cockpit vehicle with four properly positioned wheels, minimum wheelbase, rollover stability, adequate ground clearance, and no prohibited or damaging ground contact. It must accommodate a broad driver size range, protect the driver’s head and hands during rollovers, and provide required forward and side visibility. pp. 19–20V.1 Configuration

Vehicle requirements specify an open-wheeled, open-cockpit formula-style vehicle with four non-collinear wheels. Wheels must have their upper 180° unobstructed from above, remain unobstructed from the side, and be clear of defined 75 mm fore/aft wheel keep-out zones extending laterally across the wheel width. Minimum wheelbase is 1525 mm. Track and center of gravity must provide adequate rollover stability, and the smaller track must be at least 75% of the larger. Sliding skirts or intentionally designed devices contacting the track are prohibited, and intentional, excessive, or damaging/unacceptable unintended ground contact forfeits a run or potentially the entire dynamic event.

pp. 20–21V.3 Suspension and Steering

Suspension must be fully operational, include front and rear shock absorbers, and provide at least 50 mm usable wheel travel with the driver seated. Officials may disqualify inadequate or unsuitable suspension; all mounting points must be visible at inspection. Suspension fasteners are critical, and spherical suspension/steering bearings must be double-shear mounted or securely captured. Steering must mechanically connect the wheel to the front wheels through a rigid linkage capable of tension and compression; electric front-wheel steering is prohibited. Positive stops must prevent linkage lockup and wheel/tire contact, free play is limited to 7°, and the rack must be mechanically attached to the chassis. All steering joints must be visible mechanical connections; bonded-only joints are prohibited, and steering fasteners are critical. Rear-wheel steering is permitted with mechanical stops limiting movement to 6°, inspection-verifiable with the driver seated, and may be electrically actuated. The steering wheel must comply with T.1.4.4, use a quick disconnect operable while gloved from the driving position, and have a continuous near-circular or near-oval perimeter without concave sections or H, figure-eight, or cutout designs.

pp. 23–25F.1 Definitions

Defines chassis and structural terminology for Formula SAE. The chassis is the fabricated structure supporting vehicle systems; a frame member is continuous uncut tubing; a monocoque carries loads through external panels. It defines the Main Hoop, Front Hoop, Roll Hoops, their bracing supports, Front Bulkhead, and the forward deformable Impact Attenuator protecting the feet. Primary Structure includes the bulkhead and supports, roll hoops and bracing, side-impact structure, EV tractive-system and rear-impact protection, and restraint-load-transferring members. The Primary Structure Envelope follows its frame-member outline; Major Structure is the enclosed chassis portion excluding specified hoop bracing and upper Main Hoop sections. The Rollover Protection, Tire Surface, and Component Envelopes define protective geometric volumes and planes, with a rear-limit exception where no rear triangulation exists. pp. 138–140D.13 Efficiency Event

The Efficiency event evaluates fuel or energy use relative to Endurance-course lap time and distance. IC vehicles must be filled to the fuel-level line without shaking or tilting; EVs may fully charge. Vehicles complete Endurance, then power down and are pushed to fueling or data download. IC consumption is measured by refilling to the line; level changes require adding fuel and doubling that amount in measured consumption, while damaged or leaking tanks are not refilled. EV energy-meter data is downloaded, with applicable violations and penalties. Efficiency eligibility requires average lap time no more than 1.45 times the fastest eligible team’s time, consumption below specified limits, and crossing the start line after driver change; unmeasurable usage earns zero. Fuel/energy is converted to CO2: gasoline 2.31 kg/L, E85 1.65 kg/L, and electricity 0.65 kg/kWh, with full regenerative-braking credit. The formula compares per-lap CO2 and time against best eligible results; minimum thresholds are 60.06 kg CO2/100 km for IC, 20.02 for EV, and 1.45 times minimum time.
