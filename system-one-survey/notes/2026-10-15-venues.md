# Where to submit the survey (checked 2026-10-15)

A research agent checked the venue pages on 2026-10-15. "Unverified" means that the official page was not available or gave no date. "Expected" means that we use the date of the last edition. Check each venue page again before you submit.

---

## 1. Five facts that change our plan

1. **TMLR does not review surveys now.** Its FAQ says: "As of September 1, 2026, TMLR no longer considers survey papers for review."
2. **arXiv CS takes a survey only after a peer-reviewed venue accepts it.** This rule started on 2025-10-31. You must give the journal reference and the DOI. A workshop review does not count. Thus, we cannot post the survey on arXiv first.
3. **The first two deadlines that accept surveys are in January 2027:**
   - ARR (to ACL 2027): **2027-01-04**.
   - IJCAI 2027: the main track deadline is **2027-01-11**. The survey track call is not published yet. In 2026, the survey deadline was the same as the main deadline.
4. **ARR needs a qualified reviewer for each submission (from October 2026).** One author must agree to review. A PhD student qualifies with 2 or more papers at main ACL or ML venues and 3 or more papers in total. A submission without a qualified reviewer goes to a lottery, and ARR can desk-reject it.
5. **The IJCAI survey track needs a "recognized expert" as an author (2026 rule).** That author sends a 2-page CV. The track is not anonymous. Each author can send only one survey.

**Result:** For the conference routes, we need a senior co-author. Prof. Kaushik Roy is a possible person. Ask before November.

## 2. Recommendation

Do the work once. Choose the publication route at the decision point on **2026-12-01**.

| Route | When | Condition | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. ARR to ACL 2027** (8-page short version: survey plus evaluation) | Submit 2027-01-04 | One author is a qualified reviewer | Strong NLP venue. Surveys are a listed contribution type. Free. ARR permits a preprint during review. |
| **B. IJCAI 2027 survey track** (7 pages plus 2 pages of references) | Expected 2027-01-11 | A recognized expert is a co-author | A dedicated survey track at a top AI conference. |
| **C. A journal: JAIR or ACM TIST** (long version, 25 to 40 pages) | Any time from 2027-01 | None | JAIR is free and open access, and it has a Surveys section. TIST welcomes "surveys covering novel and emerging areas". |

My recommendation:

1. If a senior co-author joins, use **route A** for the short version. Then extend it into a long journal version for **ACM CSUR or TIST** after the decision.
2. If no senior co-author joins, use **route C with JAIR**. It is free, the reviews take about 8 to 12 weeks, and it permits arXiv.
3. Do not send the same paper to two venues at the same time. All these venues forbid it.

### How to get early visibility without arXiv

- Publish the benchmark code, the data and a results table on GitHub in December 2026.
- Keep a public list of System One models and papers in the same repository.
- Optional: write the evaluation as a separate **research paper** (not a review). arXiv accepts research papers without peer review. Then the survey cites it. This is two papers from one body of work, so the text must not overlap. Discuss this option at the decision point.

## 5. Rules about preprints

- **arXiv CS:** See fact 2. arXiv does not say how it treats a survey that also contains a new benchmark (unverified). ([arXiv blog, 2025-10-31](https://blog.arxiv.org/2025/10/31/attention-authors-updated-practice-for-review-articles-and-position-papers-in-arxiv-cs-category/))
- **ARR:** There is no anonymity period since 2024-02-15. You can post a preprint during the review. ([ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp))
- **ICML and NeurIPS:** You can post a preprint. Do not advertise the paper as a submission to the conference.
- **IJCAI survey track:** It is not anonymous.
- **JAIR:** It permits arXiv.

## 6. What the venues expect from a survey

The guidelines of CSUR, IJCAI and CL ask for:

- a clear scope and motivation;
- a classification of the literature (a taxonomy);
- a comparison of the approaches;
- the trends and the open problems;
- "a personal stance" of the authors;
- no bias toward the authors' own work.

No AI or NLP venue that we checked asks for PRISMA. We will still add a short method section with the sources, search strings, date range, inclusion rules and counts. This makes the review stronger.

The venues reject surveys for these reasons (quoted):

- "a simple juxtaposition of summaries of papers" (IJCAI);
- "a descriptive enumeration of the contents of papers" (TACL);
- "merely catalog existing work" (CL);
- "merely summarise a selection of existing literature without providing new insights" (JAIR);
- "little more than annotated bibliographies, with no substantial discussion of open research issues" (arXiv);
- factual errors, also in the references (IJCAI 2026 desk rejections).
